# Major Central Government Schemes for Citizens

## Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) — Housing for All

### Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana is a flagship housing scheme launched by the Government of India to provide affordable housing to the urban and rural poor. The mission aims to ensure "Housing for All" by providing financial assistance for construction or purchase of houses.

### Eligibility
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Annual household income up to ₹3 lakh.
- Low Income Group (LIG): Annual household income between ₹3 lakh to ₹6 lakh.
- Middle Income Group I (MIG-I): Annual household income between ₹6 lakh to ₹12 lakh.
- Middle Income Group II (MIG-II): Annual household income between ₹12 lakh to ₹18 lakh.
- The beneficiary family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
- The beneficiary should not have availed of any central government housing scheme benefit.

### Benefits
- EWS: Subsidy of up to ₹2.67 lakh on home loan interest.
- LIG: Subsidy of up to ₹2.67 lakh on home loan interest.
- MIG-I: Subsidy of up to ₹2.35 lakh on home loan interest.
- MIG-II: Subsidy of up to ₹2.30 lakh on home loan interest.
- Direct financial assistance of ₹1.5 lakh to ₹2.5 lakh for house construction in rural areas (PMAY-G).

### How to Apply
1. Visit the official PMAY website: https://pmaymis.gov.in
2. Click on "Citizen Assessment" → "For Slum Dwellers" or "Benefits under other 3 components".
3. Enter your Aadhaar number and verify.
4. Fill in the application form with personal, income, and property details.
5. Submit the application and note down the application ID.
6. You can also apply through Common Service Centers (CSC) or your local municipal office.

### Helpline
- Phone: 1800-11-6163 (Toll-Free)
- NHB Helpline: 1800-11-3377

## Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

### Overview
MGNREGA guarantees 100 days of wage employment per year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. It is the world's largest work guarantee program.

### Eligibility
- Any adult member of a rural household.
- Willing to do unskilled manual work.
- Must have a Job Card issued by the Gram Panchayat.

### Benefits
- Guaranteed 100 days of employment per financial year per household.
- Daily wage varies by state (approximately ₹250 to ₹350 per day).
- If employment is not provided within 15 days of application, unemployment allowance is payable.
- Wages paid directly to bank account within 15 days of work completion.

### How to Apply
1. Apply for a Job Card at your Gram Panchayat.
2. Submit application with a passport-size photograph.
3. Job Card is issued within 15 days (free of cost).
4. Once you have a Job Card, demand work in writing from the Gram Panchayat.
5. Work should be provided within 15 days, within 5 km of your village.

### Helpline
- Phone: 1800-345-22-44 (Toll-Free)
- Website: https://nrega.nic.in

## Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM) — Clean India Mission

### Overview
Swachh Bharat Mission aims to achieve universal sanitation coverage and cleanliness across India. It covers construction of individual household toilets (IHHL), community and public toilets, solid waste management, and behavior change campaigns.

### Eligibility
- SBM-Gramin (Rural): All rural households that do not have a toilet.
- SBM-Urban: All urban local bodies for public toilet construction and waste management. Individual households in urban areas without toilets.

### Benefits
- Rural: Financial incentive of ₹12,000 per household for construction of individual household latrine (IHHL).
- Urban: Financial assistance for construction of individual household toilets.
- Community and public toilet construction in urban areas.
- Solid and liquid waste management infrastructure.
- ODF (Open Defecation Free) certification for villages and cities.

### How to Apply
1. Rural: Contact your Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office.
2. Urban: Contact your nearest Urban Local Body (ULB) or Municipal Corporation.
3. Online: Apply through the SBM portal or the Swachh Bharat App.
4. Documents needed: Aadhaar card, BPL card (if applicable), photograph.

### Helpline
- Phone: 1969 (SBM Helpline)
- Website: https://swachhbharatmission.gov.in

## Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) — Har Ghar Jal

### Overview
Jal Jeevan Mission aims to provide safe and adequate drinking water through individual household tap connections by 2024 to every rural household. It focuses on ensuring Functional Household Tap Connection (FHTC) to every rural home.

### Eligibility
- All rural households across India that don't have a tap water connection.
- Priority given to SC/ST dominated habitations, villages in water quality-affected areas, and drought-prone areas.

### Benefits
- Functional household tap connection providing 55 liters per capita per day (lpcd) of drinking water.
- Water quality monitoring and surveillance.
- Community participation in water supply management.
- Greywater management and water source strengthening.

### How to Apply
1. Contact your Gram Panchayat — they manage the village-level implementation.
2. The Village Water & Sanitation Committee (VWSC) oversees the scheme locally.
3. You can report water quality issues through the Jal Jeevan Mission app.

### Helpline
- Website: https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in
- Report water issues: Jal Jeevan Mission App (available on Play Store)

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

### Overview
PM-KISAN provides direct income support of ₹6,000 per year to all landholding farmer families across India. The amount is released in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every four months.

### Eligibility
- All landholding farmer families (subject to certain exclusion criteria).
- Exclusion: Institutional landholders, former and present holders of constitutional posts, all serving or retired officers and employees of State/Central Government, professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and income tax payers.

### Benefits
- ₹6,000 per year in 3 installments of ₹2,000 each.
- Amount directly transferred to the farmer's bank account.
- Installment periods: April-July, August-November, December-March.

### How to Apply
1. Visit the PM-KISAN portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click on "New Farmer Registration".
3. Enter Aadhaar number and state.
4. Fill in the registration form with land details and bank account.
5. Submit and note the registration number.
6. You can also register through Common Service Centers or your local agriculture office.

### Helpline
- Phone: 155261 or 011-24300606
- Website: https://pmkisan.gov.in

## Ayushman Bharat — Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

### Overview
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health insurance scheme providing health coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. It covers over 50 crore beneficiaries.

### Eligibility
- Families identified based on deprivation and occupational criteria from the SECC 2011 database.
- Rural: Families with deprivation criteria like kutcha house, no adult earning member, SC/ST households, landless manual casual laborers.
- Urban: Workers in categories like rag pickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, plumbers, painters, security guards, etc.

### Benefits
- Health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year.
- Covers 1,929+ treatment packages including surgery, medical treatments, and day care.
- Cashless and paperless treatment at empaneled hospitals.
- Covers pre and post hospitalization expenses.
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender.
- Pre-existing diseases covered from day one.

### How to Apply
1. Check eligibility: Visit https://mera.pmjay.gov.in or call 14555.
2. Enter your mobile number or ration card number to check if you're eligible.
3. If eligible, visit your nearest Common Service Center (CSC) or empaneled hospital.
4. Carry Aadhaar card, ration card, and any government ID for verification.
5. An Ayushman Bharat e-card will be generated for you.

### Helpline
- Phone: 14555 (Toll-Free)
- Website: https://pmjay.gov.in

## Ujjwala Yojana — Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

### Overview
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana provides free LPG connections to women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) families. The scheme aims to replace unclean cooking fuels like firewood, coal, and dung cakes with clean LPG fuel.

### Eligibility
- Women belonging to BPL households.
- No existing LPG connection in the name of any household member.
- Applicant must be above 18 years of age.
- Under PMUY 2.0: Migrant workers, households without LPG, and those identified through SECC data.

### Benefits
- Free LPG connection (security deposit for cylinder and regulator waived).
- Free first LPG refill and stove.
- EMI facility available for purchasing a stove and first refill.

### How to Apply
1. Visit your nearest LPG distributor (HP Gas, Bharat Gas, or Indane).
2. Fill in the Ujjwala application form (KYC form).
3. Submit: Aadhaar card, BPL card or SECC list proof, photograph, and bank account details.
4. The connection will be issued in the name of the adult woman of the household.

### Helpline
- Phone: 1800-266-6696 (Toll-Free)
- Website: https://www.pmuy.gov.in

## National Pension System (NPS) — Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

### Overview
Atal Pension Yojana is a government-backed pension scheme for workers in the unorganized sector. It provides a guaranteed minimum monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after age 60, depending on the contribution.

### Eligibility
- Indian citizens between 18 to 40 years of age.
- Must have a savings bank account.
- Not an income tax payer.
- Not a member of any statutory social security scheme.

### Benefits
- Guaranteed monthly pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 after 60 years of age.
- Government co-contributes 50% of the premium (up to ₹1,000/year) for 5 years for those who joined before March 2016.
- Spouse continues to receive pension after subscriber's death.
- Nominee receives the accumulated pension corpus.

### How to Apply
1. Visit your bank or post office where you have a savings account.
2. Fill in the APY registration form.
3. Provide Aadhaar number and mobile number.
4. Choose pension amount (₹1,000 to ₹5,000/month).
5. Auto-debit will be set up from your bank account.
6. You can also enroll through net banking or mobile banking apps.

### Helpline
- Phone: 1800-110-069 (Toll-Free)
- Website: https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in
